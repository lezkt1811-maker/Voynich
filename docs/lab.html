Voynich Cipher Lab

A workbench for testing a personal EVA cipher. It keeps the manuscript text, the rules you propose, and your hypotheses in separate places.

The Voynich Manuscript has not been deciphered. Nothing this lab produces is a translation. Every reading shows the rule that made it, and any meaning you write is your hypothesis, not a finding.
